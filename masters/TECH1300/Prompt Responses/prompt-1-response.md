## Paragraph formula

Use this structure for most body paragraphs:

1. **Claim** — state the main point.
2. **Evidence** — introduce a reliable source.
3. **Analysis** — explain why the evidence matters.
4. **Critical point** — identify a limitation, trade-off or contrasting perspective.
5. **Link** — connect to the next paragraph.

Example:

> Effective IS planning requires alignment between business objectives, processes and system requirements, rather than a technology-first decision (Pant & Hsu, 1995). This alignment reduces the risk of implementing systems that automate inefficient processes. However, planning alone is insufficient unless it remains connected to implementation activities, including stakeholder engagement, skills development and process integration (Guntur et al., 2018).
